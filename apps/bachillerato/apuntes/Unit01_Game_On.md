## 🏅 Core Sports Vocabulary

| English Term | Part of Speech | Spanish Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Award** | *noun / verb* | Premio / Conceder un premio | *He received an award for his outstanding athletic achievements.* |
| **Winner** | *noun* | Ganador / Vencedor | *The winner of the tournament will qualify for the finals.* |
| **Break a record** | *collocation* | Batir / Romper un récord | *Armand Duplantis managed to break the pole vault world record.* |
| **Championship** | *noun* | Campeonato / Torneo | *They trained hard all summer for the national championship.* |
| **Defeat** | *noun / verb* | Derrota / Derrotar | *They refused to accept defeat and kept pushing until the end.* |
| **Beat** | *verb* | Vencer / Derrotar a alguien | *Spain beat England in the Euro finals.* *(beat + rival vs. win + trophy/match)* |
| **Perform** | *verb* | Rendir / Actuar / Desempeñarse | *Athletes must perform under extreme psychological pressure.* |
| **Runner-up** | *noun* | Subcampeón / Segundo puesto | *Although he was the runner-up, his performance was remarkable.* |
| **Score** | *noun / verb* | Marcador / Anotar / Marcar | *He scored the winning goal in the final minute of the match.* |
| **Spectator** | *noun* | Espectador (en estadio/evento) | *Thousands of spectators gathered to watch the opening ceremony.* |
| **Take up** | *phrasal verb* | Empezar a practicar (hobby/deporte)| *She decided to take up tennis to improve her endurance.* |
| **Opponent** | *noun* | Oponente / Adversario / Rival | *Respecting your opponent is a core value of fair play.* |

## 🎯 Advanced Vocabulary (Exercise 5, Page 6)

1. **Thrilled** *(adj.)*: Extremely pleased and excited (*"He was thrilled to be selected for the Olympic team"*).
2. **Attached** *(adj.)*: Emotionally connected to something / joined (*"He became deeply attached to his coach's advice"*).
3. **Ban** *(verb / noun)*: Officially prohibit something (*"The committee decided to ban performance-enhancing substances"*).
4. **Scruffy** *(adj.)*: Untidy, messy or worn-out in appearance (*"He showed up in scruffy training clothes"*).
5. **Tough** *(adj.)*: Difficult, requiring great determination or physically strong (*"It was a tough match, but he stayed resilient"*).

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls for the Exam
- **Win vs. Beat:**
  - *Win:* You win a prize, a game, a trophy, a medal, an award (*"He won the gold medal"*).
  - *Beat:* You beat an opponent, a person, or an opposing team (*"Duplantis beat his competitors"*).
  - ❌ *He won his opponent.*
  - ✅ *He beat his opponent.*
- **Spectator vs. Audience vs. Viewer:**
  - *Spectator:* Live sporting event in a stadium.
  - *Audience:* Theatre, concert, cinema, or lecture.
  - *Viewer:* Television or streaming content at home.
